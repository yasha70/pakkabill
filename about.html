About PakkaBill

Know your real profit · अपना असली मुनाफ़ा जानिए

PakkaBill is made for small Indian sellers: Meesho, Amazon and Flipkart sellers, boutiques, and shops that make GST bills. Most sellers see their sales go up but never know what they really earn after marketplace fees, returns, RTO, GST and the cost of the product. PakkaBill shows that number, reconciled to the paisa, in about two minutes.

Meesho Profit & LossUpload the Meesho payment report and see your real profit, by month, by product and by category, with RTO, returns and exchanges shown separately.
GST bills in secondsTax invoices and estimates with CGST, SGST and IGST worked out, shared on WhatsApp or printed.
GST returns helpGST summary and a GSTR-1 file from your marketplace reports, ready for the GST portal.
Private by designYour reports are read on your own phone or computer. No ads, and we never sell your data.

Why we built it

Sales are not profit. A seller can sell a lot on Meesho and still lose money once RTO parcels, return shipping and fees are taken out. We wanted every small seller to have the kind of clear profit and loss statement that big brands get from their accountants, in simple words, in English and Hindi, at a price anyone can afford.

How it works

  1. Download your payment report from the Meesho Supplier Panel (Payments → Download).
  2. Open PakkaBill → Profit & Loss and upload it.
  3. Add what each product costs you once. PakkaBill remembers it.
  4. See your real profit, and download it as a PDF or Excel report.

There is a step-by-step guide in English and Hindi inside the tool (How to use tab).
