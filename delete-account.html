Delete your PakkaBill account

You can delete your account in the app (PakkaBill → My account → Delete my account) or right here.

What is deleted

  • Your login (mobile number and password) and your plan, including any Pro time left.
  • Every shop saved in cloud backup: bills, parties, items and settings.
  • Phone notification settings for your account.

What is kept

  • Payment records (amount, date, UPI transaction number), for as long as Indian tax law requires.
  • Help requests you sent us (ask us in Help & support and we will delete them too).
  • Bills saved on your own phone or computer stay there until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.

Delete now

Forgot your password? Open Help & support in PakkaBill and ask us to delete your account; we will check it is your number and delete it within 7 days. See also our privacy policy.
