PakkaBill

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026. This policy covers the PakkaBill website (pakkabill1.vercel.app) and the PakkaBill app for Android.

In short: your bills stay on your phone unless you turn on cloud backup. We do not sell your data, we show no ads and we use no third-party tracking or analytics tools; we only count visits with our own simple counter (see section 2). You can download all your data and delete your account at any time.

1. What PakkaBill keeps on your device

The bills, estimates, parties (buyers and suppliers), items, shop details, logo and settings you enter are saved on your own phone or computer, inside PakkaBill. The files you upload to the Meesho tools (payment reports, order reports) are read on your device and are not sent to us.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, SMS or call logs. The Android app asks for no special permissions apart from the optional fingerprint/face app lock, which is checked by your phone; PakkaBill never sees your fingerprint or face.

3. Who processes the data

PakkaBill runs on Vercel (hosting) and Upstash (database). All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts. If AI answers are switched on in Help, the question you type is sent to Anthropic to write the answer. Payments are made directly between your UPI app and our bank account; we never see your UPI PIN or bank details. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and cloud backups: until you delete them or your account.
  • Login sessions: up to 180 days, or until you log out.
  • Payment records: as long as Indian tax law requires (up to 8 years), even after the account is deleted.
  • Help requests: kept so we can follow up on problems; ask us in Help & support and we will delete them.

5. Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: PakkaBill → My account → Download all my data.
  • Correct it: edit your shop, bills, parties and items in the app at any time.
  • Stop cloud backup: Shops & cloud → turn off automatic backup, or delete a shop's backup.
  • Delete your account: PakkaBill → My account → Delete my account, or on the account deletion page. This removes your login, plan and all cloud backups.

6. Children

PakkaBill is a business tool for sellers aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, logins use random session keys, all traffic is encrypted, and each account can only read its own data. No system is perfect; if we ever find a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and show a note in the app. For any privacy question or complaint (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), write to us through Help & support in PakkaBill or contact our grievance officer (details on the Contact us page). We aim to reply within 7 days.
