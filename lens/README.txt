PakkaBill Lens - Meesho insights, auto listing and shipping check (Chrome extension, version 3.5)

Works in Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. On a phone, use the Lens bookmark
from PakkaBill's Meesho Lens page instead (phone Chrome can't run extensions).

INSTALL
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome (edge://extensions in Edge).
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped pakkabill-lens folder.
5. Open any product on meesho.com. The Lens panel appears at the bottom right.
6. Open https://pakkabill1.vercel.app/#/lens to see every competitor you opened, side by side.

WHAT IS REAL AND WHAT IS ESTIMATED
Real (read from the page, marked "real"; "checked" when two parts of the page agree):
Meesho price, crossed-out price, delivery, price by size, rating, ratings and reviews,
rating breakdown, seller name, followers, products listed, fabric, pack size.
Estimated (marked "estimate"): seller price (price minus the shipping Meesho adds),
bank settlement (minus TCS 0.5% and TDS 0.1%), earning after GST, orders (ratings x
"orders per 1 rating"), orders per day (growth in ratings), return risk (share of poor
and average ratings). Meesho does not publish anyone's real orders or return %.

ORDERS PER DAY
Each product you open is saved on your computer, one reading per day. Open the same
product again on a later day and the Lens shows new ratings per day and estimated
orders per day, using about the last week of readings.

SETTINGS
Open "Settings for estimates" to set shipping, GST %, orders per rating, your cost per
piece, your returns % and loss per return. PakkaBill's Meesho Lens page shares them.

SHIPPING CHECK (LOW SHIPPING IMAGE) - EXACT SHIPPING FROM MEESHO
1. In PakkaBill's Image tab (Low shipping), choose up to 5 categories, upload your product
   photo and tap "Generate images". PakkaBill makes versions (framed, stickers, slight zoom,
   turn, light, white background) and sends them to the Lens with your categories.
2. In the same browser, open supplier.meesho.com -> Catalog Upload -> Add Single Catalog.
   Pick a category and upload your own photo once by hand, as you always do, until Meesho
   shows a shipping charge.
3. The PakkaBill panel (bottom left) says "Learned: Meesho priced your photo at Rs X". It
   learnt how Meesho's own page asks for the shipping price (from your upload, in your tab).
4. The check starts by itself (or tap "Check all"). First your own photo is sent again; it must come back at the same Rs X,
   or nothing else is checked. Then each version is sent to Meesho the same way, once per
   category, and the shipping is read from Meesho's own answer, so it is the exact number
   Meesho would show. Results appear in PakkaBill live: shipping rate per category on every
   image, cheapest first, and a Download button.
5. If it cannot learn (Meesho changed its page), open "Not working? Other ways": "Read from
   Meesho's screen" puts each version in the photo box and reads the charge shown, and
   "Copy a report for support" copies only the shape of the requests (no photos, no tokens,
   no cookies) to send to PakkaBill support.
Versions with no answer are tried once more. If Meesho says you are logged out, the check
stops: log in, upload your photo again and tap Check all.
No extension (phone): PakkaBill's Image tab also has a "PB Shipping" bookmark that does the same
check inside your Meesho tab and sends the prices back to PakkaBill.
It works only in the Meesho tab you have open, in your own account, with your own login.
It never submits a catalogue and never saves or sends your login anywhere.

AUTO LISTING (FILL MEESHO'S CATALOGUE FORM IN ONE CLICK)
1. Save a template once. Easiest: in supplier.meesho.com -> Catalog Upload -> Add Single
   Catalog, fill one product by hand (category, details, sizes, prices, stock), then open the
   PakkaBill panel (bottom right) and tap "Save this form as a template". Or make templates in
   PakkaBill's Auto listing page (https://pakkabill1.vercel.app/#/autolist).
2. In PakkaBill's Auto listing, add products: photos, name, colour, SKU, and price or stock if
   they differ. Tap "Send to Meesho".
3. In Meesho, open Add Single Catalog, pick the category, and tap "Fill this form" in the
   PakkaBill panel. Every detail, the sizes with price, MRP, stock and SKU, the product name,
   description and photos are filled. Check the form and submit it in Meesho, then tap
   "Submitted, next" for the next product. PakkaBill shows each product as submitted.
4. Tap "Read all drop-downs" once per category. PakkaBill opens every drop-down, reads all
   its choices and saves them on the template, so PakkaBill's Auto listing offers Meesho's
   exact choices for each detail. When filling, a value whose label is different in Meesho goes
   to the drop-down that has that choice, so you do not need to point at anything.
5. If something is still not filled, tap "Copy form report" and send it to PakkaBill support.
   It has the form's labels and boxes only: no prices, photos or login. ("Point" still works.)
6. In PakkaBill's Auto listing: type "Your details for every listing" once (manufacturer, packer,
   country of origin, brand); "+ Colours" turns one product into one per colour with its own SKU;
   "Write name and description" makes them from the template's details.
7. After filling, the panel lists Meesho's required (*) boxes that are still empty and adds them to
   the template, so the next product fills them too. Keys: Alt+Shift+F fill, Alt+Shift+N next.
It fills only the form on the page you have open, in your own account, and never presses
Submit.

The extension reads only pages you open. It does not crawl Meesho and sends nothing
to any server; PakkaBill gets your list only when you open its Meesho Lens page.
