Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026. Applies to PakkaBill Pro bought on the PakkaBill website or the app downloaded from it.

In short: Pro never renews by itself, so there is nothing to cancel. If you paid by mistake, paid twice, or Pro did not work for you, ask within 7 days and we refund the full amount to the UPI account you paid from.

1. Cancellation

PakkaBill Pro is a prepaid plan (₹99 a month or ₹999 a year). It is not an automatic subscription: we never take money without you paying again yourself. When your plan ends, your account moves to the free plan; your bills, shops and data stay safe.

2. When you get a full refund

  • You paid twice for the same plan, or paid more than the price shown.
  • Your payment reached us but Pro was not switched on within 3 working days.
  • You ask within 7 days of paying for a new plan, because Pro did not work as described for you.
  • We stop a paid feature you have paid for (you get the unused part back).

3. When a refund is not given

  • More than 7 days after payment, for a plan that worked as described. The plan simply runs until its end date.
  • Free trials and free days from offers (nothing was paid).
  • Accounts closed for breaking the Terms, such as making fake invoices.

4. How to ask

Open PakkaBill → Help → Payment and Pro, or write to us (see Contact us), with your mobile number and the UPI transaction number (UTR). We reply within 2 working days.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the same UPI account or bank account the payment came from, within 5 to 7 working days. If a payment could not be matched to your account (for example a wrong UTR), we check it with our bank and refund it or switch Pro on, whichever you prefer.

6. Payments you did not make

If you see a payment to PakkaBill that you did not make, tell your bank and contact us at once.

Pro is a digital service; nothing is shipped, so there is no shipping or delivery policy. Pro is available right after the payment is confirmed.
